MODELLING CONVENTIONS

Assumptions stay explicit.

Revenue growth is applied for five forecast years. EBITDA margin drives operating profit. D&A, capital expenditure and working-capital investment determine cash generation.

Taxes apply to positive EBIT. Tax benefits from losses are not assumed. Net income is not forecast without financing and other below-EBIT schedules.

WACC must exceed terminal growth. Negative net debt represents net cash.

This is an industrial-company FCFF model. Banks and insurers require a sector-appropriate equity valuation method.
